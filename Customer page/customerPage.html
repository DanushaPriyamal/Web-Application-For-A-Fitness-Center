💪 How to Build a Workout Routine That Works

Quick tips to help you stay consistent and reach your fitness goals.

🎯 1. Set Your Goals

Decide what you’re aiming for:

  • Muscle gain
  • Fat loss
  • Cardio endurance
  • Flexibility or mobility

Your goals shape your routine.

🗓️ 2. Weekly Workout Split

Day Focus
Monday Upper Body Strength
Tuesday Cardio & Core
Wednesday Lower Body Strength
Thursday Active Recovery
Friday Full Body Circuit
Saturday Cardio Endurance
Sunday Rest

🔁 3. Balance Your Training

  • Strength: 3–4 sessions/week with compound lifts
  • Cardio: Mix HIIT and steady-state
  • Flexibility: Stretch, foam roll, or do yoga weekly

📈 4. Progress Over Time

Increase weights, reps, or intensity gradually to keep improving.

🥗 5. Support with Nutrition & Recovery

  • Eat balanced meals
  • Stay hydrated
  • Get 7–9 hours of sleep

🥗 Healthy Recipes to Fuel Your Fitness Goals

Eating clean doesn’t mean boring meals. These simple, nutrient-packed recipes are perfect for anyone looking to support their workouts with smart nutrition.

🍳 1. Protein-Packed Breakfast: Veggie Omelette

Ingredients:

  • 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg
  • Spinach, tomatoes, onions (chopped)
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil

Instructions: Sauté veggies in olive oil, add eggs, cook until firm. Serve with whole grain toast.

🥗 2. Light Lunch: Grilled Chicken Salad

Ingredients:

  • Grilled chicken breast (sliced)
  • Mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
  • Olive oil + lemon juice dressing

Instructions: Toss all ingredients together. Add dressing and enjoy fresh.

🍠 3. Post-Workout Snack: Sweet Potato & Greek Yogurt

Ingredients:

  • 1 medium baked sweet potato
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • Cinnamon and honey (optional)

Instructions: Slice sweet potato, top with yogurt, sprinkle cinnamon and drizzle honey if desired.

🍲 4. Dinner Idea: Quinoa Stir-Fry

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • Broccoli, bell peppers, carrots
  • Low-sodium soy sauce, garlic, olive oil

Instructions: Stir-fry veggies in olive oil and garlic, add quinoa and soy sauce. Cook for 5 minutes and serve hot.

🥤 5. Hydration Boost: Green Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup spinach
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup almond milk

Instructions: Blend all ingredients until smooth. Great for mornings or post-workout refreshment.
